Pardot Toolkit — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pardot Toolkit — Bulk Mappings & Deliverability ("Pardot Toolkit", "the Extension", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Chrome extension.

1. Purpose of the Extension

Pardot Toolkit is a Chrome extension for Salesforce Account Engagement (Pardot) users. It provides tools for managing form-handler field mappings, searching supported Pardot/Salesforce pages, generating deliverability-related reports, and displaying synchronization-error notifications.

2. Information the Extension Can Access

The Extension requests browser permissions needed to operate on supported Pardot and Salesforce pages. These include storage, active-tab access, notifications, and access to the supported Pardot/Salesforce web pages listed in the Extension's manifest.

Depending on the feature you use, the Extension may process information that is already available in the supported webpage, such as form-handler configuration, field mappings, user/handler information, and synchronization or deliverability information.

3. How Information Is Used

Information accessed by the Extension is used only to provide the Extension's features, such as displaying tools, processing requested mappings or reports, searching information available to you, and showing notifications.

4. Data Storage

The Extension may use Chrome's storage functionality for extension settings or other information required to operate its features. The Extension does not intentionally create a separate advertising or analytics profile about users.

5. Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell personal information. We do not intentionally share information accessed by the Extension with advertisers or data brokers.

6. Remote Code

The Extension does not download or execute remote JavaScript or other executable code. Its executable extension code is packaged with the Extension.

7. Third-Party Services

The Extension operates with websites and services that you choose to access, including Salesforce Account Engagement (Pardot) and related Salesforce domains. Your use of those services is governed by their respective privacy policies and terms.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to limit the Extension's access to the permissions necessary for its stated functionality. No software or Internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

The Extension is intended for business users and is not directed toward children under 13.
