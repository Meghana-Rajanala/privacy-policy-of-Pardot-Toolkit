Privacy Policy: Pardot Toolkit

Effective date: October 10, 2026

Pardot Toolkit ("the extension") is a browser extension published by [YOUR NAME OR COMPANY]. This policy explains what the extension does with information.

Summary

The extension runs entirely inside your browser. It does not collect, store on our servers, sell, or share your data. We have no servers that receive data from the extension.

What the extension accesses

To do its job, the extension reads and edits the content of Account Engagement (Pardot) pages you open, namely the field list on form handler pages. It also requests Pardot search pages from your own Pardot address using your existing logged-in session, and shows the links it finds in the popup. CSV text you paste or load into the Batch tab is processed in your browser only.

What is stored

The extension stores the following in your browser's local extension storage, on your device only: your settings (Pardot address, URL template, speed options, default for the Required checkbox, notification options, search URL templates), the last form handler ID you used, and the progress and results of your most recent batch run. You can clear this by changing the settings, removing the extension, or clearing the extension's data in Chrome.

What is not collected

We do not collect personal information, browsing history, credentials, analytics, or usage statistics. We do not use cookies or trackers. The extension does not send data to the developer or to any third party.

Network requests

The only network requests the extension makes are to the Pardot / Salesforce address you configure, to open form handler pages and run searches as you, the logged-in user. These requests go directly from your browser to your Pardot account.

Permissions

storage (save settings locally), tabs (find and open form handler pages), scripting (attach the helper script to open Pardot tabs), notifications (tell you when a batch finishes), and access to pardot.com, salesforce.com and force.com pages (read and edit form handler fields on those pages).

Children

The extension is a business tool and is not directed at children.
